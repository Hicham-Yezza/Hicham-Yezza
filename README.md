# Hicham Yezza

Principal Data Scientist for Responsible AI at the BBC, where I lead work on evaluating the accuracy, risks and safety of AI systems and their impact on BBC organisational and editorial values. I'm also a Visiting Fellow at the University of Reading.

## Current work

- Lead author of the BBC/EBU [News Integrity in AI Assistants Toolkit](https://www.ebu.ch/research/open/report/news-integrity-in-ai-assistants-toolkit), which sets out what an accurate, well-sourced AI answer looks like and how to test for one.
- Designing evaluation suites and harnesses for LLM-based tools, including checking automated (LLM-as-judge) evaluators against expert human judgement.
- Multilingual evaluation: how AI systems perform outside English.

Most of my code is Python: evaluation pipelines, NLP and data analysis.

## Background

A computer scientist by training (University of Nottingham), with more than two decades of editorial leadership and consulting work for UN agencies and NGOs.
